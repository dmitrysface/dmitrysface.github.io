Biography

Dmitry Leeguy (also written Dmitriy Ligay) is an ethnic Korean born in Uzbekistan, where he has spent almost his whole life. His photography career took place in Tashkent. Music videos as an artist, and as co-producer of CYN, were shot in Korea. He owns the NOIR venue in Tashkent and is a co-founder of the logistics company VERTEX LINE.
